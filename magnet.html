4 + 10

4 sq ft inside. 10 sq ft of run. Per bird. That's the fight on every chicken forum.

Extension-service numbers, rounded up. Not the 2 sq ft prefab that looks cute on Amazon until week six.

Roost bar is the other constraint: 8–10 inches per bird. Nesting boxes: one per 3–4 hens, and they will still queue for the same box. Adults rarely need a heat lamp — damp air kills, not cold.

Use it in 10 minutes. Count standard birds. Multiply. If the coop is the run, double the interior. Bantams: 2 + 8 instead of 4 + 10.
