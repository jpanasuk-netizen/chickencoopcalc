How Big Should a Duck House Be for 4 Ducks?

Quick answer: For 4 ducks, Penn State Extension's 3 sq ft per bird inside and 15 sq ft of run comes to 12 sq ft of house floor and 60 sq ft of run. Poultry Extension (extension.org) allows 5 sq ft per adult duck indoors, which is 20 sq ft. A 4 x 5 ft house meets both figures.

The 4-duck column is the per-duck figure times 4. extension.org does not publish an outdoor number.

Why 4 x 5 ft

4 ft × 5 ft = 20 sq ft. That equals the 20 sq ft extension.org figure for 4 ducks, so it also clears the 12 sq ft minimum. A 3 × 4 ft house is 12 sq ft: it meets the minimum but not the 5 sq ft figure. Enter your own size in the checker above.
