What Size Hardware Cloth Is Best for a Chicken Coop?

Mesh size from the extensions, then how much roll your run, walls and apron take. Prices are not printed.

Built by Jeremy Panasuk. Published November 24, 2026.

Quick answer: The best hardware cloth for a chicken coop is 1/4- to 1/2-inch mesh, says Utah State University Extension, which notes chicken wire is not a predator barrier. An Ask Extension expert suggests 1/2-inch cloth plus a 24-inch apron laid flat around the coop, and Maryland Extension says to bury it 12 inches to 1.5 feet deep.

Limits

The square footage covers flat runs of cloth; it does not add overlap at seams, doors or the corners of a buried trench. Gates and the coop's own vents need cloth too.
