Methodology & Editorial Standards

Last updated: October 6, 2026

How the calculator works

The calculator applies the rates named on the homepage in your browser. Standard birds use 4 sq ft and 10 sq ft, inside the published 3–4 and 10 figures. Bantam 2 and 8, and one nest per 3 hens, are tighter than the Penn State, Maryland, and Utah tables. Feed uses the quarter-pound figure from Alabama and Kentucky Extension. It is a planning estimate, not a measured result.

You enter a bird count and, where the page asks, a size class or another listed input. The page multiplies that count by the rate above and shows coop square feet, run square feet, nesting boxes, or feed. Amazon links on this site are affiliate links; they do not change the math.

Where the numbers come from

Housing and feed rates are the figures already linked from the homepage and the flock-size page:

  • eXtension: Space allowances in housing for small and backyard poultry flocks
  • UC Master Gardeners of Monterey Bay: Backyard chickens (2017)
  • Penn State Extension: Small Scale Poultry Housing
  • University of Maryland Extension: Housing Requirements
  • Utah State University Extension: Housing Backyard Chickens
  • Alabama Cooperative Extension: Feeding the Laying Hen
  • University of Kentucky Extension ASC-191: How Much Will My Chickens Eat?

The breed chart copies values exactly as published. Every row links its source document. The access date on that page is October 6, 2026. A missing value says "not listed". Nothing in the chart is estimated. Large-fowl weights are from University of Wisconsin–Madison Extension, Chicken Breeds & Varieties (A2880). Bantam rows use these Livestock Conservancy pages:

  • Belgian Bearded d'Uccle Bantam
  • Booted Bantam Chicken
  • Japanese Bantam Chicken
  • Nankin Chicken
  • Sebright Chicken
