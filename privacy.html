Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

ChickenCoopCalc (chickencoopcalc.com) is a free calculator site owned by Jeremy Panasuk, New Auburn, Wisconsin. It is built to collect as little information as possible.

Information we collect

The calculator runs in your browser. A shared result puts the inputs in the page address, and that address is sent when the page is requested. The site does not keep an account or a stored copy of those inputs. We do not require accounts and do not collect names, emails, or personal profiles through the calculator.

Script files this site hosts:

  • assets/app.js does the coop, feed, nest, and winter math in the page.
  • assets/monetization.js is on the homepage. It fills Amazon links after a calculation. If you dismiss the Amazon bar, it stores that dismissal in sessionStorage for the tab. That flag is not sent to us.
  • assets/magnet-cta.js is on the homepage and the topic map. It adds a link to the printable coop card and a link to the TikTok profile @3jsa7.
  • assets/breed-sort.js sorts the breed chart in the browser.
  • assets/share.js writes calculator inputs into the page address and copies text to the clipboard. It does not add its own request beyond that page address.

Advertising

This site does not load Google AdSense or any other display-ad script.

Affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links to Amazon.com through the Amazon Associates program. Those links use the tag generatorsi0d-20. If you follow one and buy something, the site earns a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links do not change the calculator math. After you leave this site, Amazon may set its own cookies under Amazon's policies.

Analytics and cookies

We do not run our own analytics and do not set our own tracking cookies. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings.
