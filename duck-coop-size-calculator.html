Duck Coop Size Calculator

Quick answer: Penn State Extension and University of Maryland Extension set ducks at 3 sq ft of house floor and 15 sq ft of outside run per bird, and Poultry Extension (extension.org) allows 5 sq ft indoors per adult duck. Multiply by your flock: 10 ducks need 30 to 50 sq ft inside and 150 sq ft of run.

Which figure to build to

The 3 sq ft line is a minimum from the Penn State and Maryland tables. The 5 sq ft line from extension.org is the larger indoor allowance, the same figure the duck space page leads with. Building between the two is still above the minimum; building to 5 sq ft meets both. The run figure is 15 sq ft per duck from both extension tables.
