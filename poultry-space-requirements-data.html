Poultry space requirements per bird

Penn State and University of Maryland Extension list minimum space of 1.5 sq ft inside and 8 sq ft of run per laying hen, 2 and 10 for large chickens and 1 and 4 for bantams. The national Poultry Extension guide recommends more: 3 to 4 sq ft indoors and 10 sq ft outdoors per hen.

Last updated: October 10, 2026.

Built by Jeremy Panasuk.

Penn State Extension and University of Maryland Extension publish the same minimums (both trace to Clauer), so they appear as separate rows and each source stands on its own. The national Poultry Extension guide recommends more space. Both are shown side by side; this page does not pick one number.
