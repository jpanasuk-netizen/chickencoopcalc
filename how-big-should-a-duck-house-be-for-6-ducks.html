How Big Should a Duck House Be for 6 Ducks?

Quick answer: Six adult ducks need 30 sq ft indoors at the eXtension rate of 5 sq ft per duck. Penn State Extension and Maryland Extension list a lower indoor minimum of 3 sq ft, which is 18 sq ft for six, and a run of 15 sq ft per duck, which is 90 sq ft. eXtension does not publish an outdoor number; it says the larger the run, the better.

Six ducks are not six hens. Hen pages on this site use 4 sq ft indoors, which sits inside the eXtension 3-4 sq ft range and the Minnesota Extension 3 to 5 sq ft range, and 10 sq ft of run from eXtension. Duck rates in this set are the 5 sq ft indoor figure from eXtension and the 3 and 15 sq ft minimums from Penn State Extension and Maryland Extension. The hen run is not a duck figure.

Moisture is the part the square footage does not say by itself. eXtension puts waterfowl droppings above 90% moisture, so a 30 sq ft house still has a wet floor to plan for. The same paragraph says that if the climate does not allow year-round outdoor use, the interior space requirement increases, and it does not say by how many feet. Geese are out of scope here: eXtension puts them at about 3 times the duck allowance.

How to size it

Multiply the published per-duck figures. Do not borrow a chicken roost length; these sources do not give ducks a perch allowance.

  1. Indoor, eXtension. 6 × 5 = 30 sq ft (eXtension).
  2. Indoor minimum. 6 × 3 = 18 sq ft (Penn State Extension, Maryland Extension).
  3. Run minimum. 6 × 15 = 90 sq ft (Penn State Extension, Maryland Extension). eXtension still says the larger the run, the better, and does not cap that advice with a second number.
  4. Example footprint. 30 sq ft is 5 × 6 ft. 90 sq ft is 6 × 15 ft.

FAQ

How big should a duck house be for 6 ducks?

eXtension lists 5 sq ft indoors per adult duck, so 6 ducks need 30 sq ft. Penn State and Maryland list a lower indoor minimum of 3 sq ft, which is 18 sq ft for 6, and 15 sq ft of run, which is 90 sq ft.

How big should the outdoor run be for 6 ducks?

Penn State and Maryland list 15 sq ft of run per duck, so 6 times 15 is 90 sq ft. eXtension does not give a specific outdoor figure and says the larger the run, the better.

Why does duck housing stay wet?

eXtension says waterfowl droppings are more than 90 percent moisture. That is a floor and bedding problem on top of the square footage. The same source gives geese about 3 times the duck allowance, which this page does not apply to ducks.

What footprint is 30 sq ft?

A 5 by 6 ft floor is 30 sq ft. A 6 by 15 ft run is 90 sq ft. Those are examples of the products, not required shapes.
